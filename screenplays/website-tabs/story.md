# Screenplay: tabs and panes

The website's tabs-and-panes clip, from the website video kit.

Trunk main links to Sewer check and Design basis. Ctrl-click Sewer check to open it in a tab, Ctrl+R to come back, then open Design basis. Right-click Sewer check in the sidebar and move it to the right pane. Back in Trunk main, write the finding under the next step: "C-03 is the slowest at 0.55 m/s."

Recorded in Retro 82 at 1680x1000 with a still camera, no logo and no closing fade; `themes` recolours it into every theme for the site. The conduit data is illustrative.
