# D7 flow basis

D7 is the 6.73 MLD SBR at the outfall. The design brief lists 6.73 MLD as the **average dry weather flow** for the 2055 population of 52,400 at 135 lpcd with 80% return.

The SBR schedule in the estimate sizes the decanters and blowers for **peak** flow, not average. The hydraulic sheet in the workbook uses a peak factor of 3.0 on the trunk main into D7.

Open question: does the ₹32.7 cr civil and E&M line price the tanks for 6.73 MLD or for 3 × 6.73 MLD?
