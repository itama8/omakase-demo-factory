# Demo factory visual identity

## Style Prompt
Preserve the existing Omakase composition: quiet deep teal canvas, a restrained logo introduction, and the actual app as the subject. Review controls should feel like an editing room around the footage, with readable labels and a warm, understated editorial heading.

## Colors
- `#07171d`: composition and review canvas (existing template).
- `#02090d`: closing curtain and video well (existing template).
- `#f4f0e7`: review text, warm ivory.
- `#bfe7d2`: review controls and active selection, pale mint.
- `#294249`: review separators and control borders.

## Typography
- Existing composition: bundled IBM Plex Serif.
- Caption pills: system sans-serif, white text on deep teal.
- Review: system sans-serif controls, Georgia editorial headings.

## What NOT to Do
- Replace actual app footage with recreated UI.
- Place captions over the content they explain when another position works.
- Add decorative motion or music that obscures the workflow.
- Hide failed interactions behind timing pauses.
